\begin{rSection}{Biographical Sketch}
    \vspace{-1em}
\item Dr. Liu is a Professor at the School of Control and Computer Engineering, North China Electric Power University (NCEPU). Before joining NCEPU in 2026, he was an Associate Professor at the State Key Laboratory of Processors (SKLP), Institute of Computing Technology (ICT), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). He received his Ph.D. degree from The University of Hong Kong in 2016, and his Master's and Bachelor's degrees from Harbin Institute of Technology in 2009 and 2007, respectively.

Dr. Liu's research focuses on reconfigurable and reliable architectures for AI computing systems, with particular emphasis on large-scale spatial architectures, domain-specific accelerators, and hardware--software co-design. More recently, his work has extended to LLM-driven hardware design automation, including hardware code generation, code-generation agents, and automated SoC design. His work addresses key challenges in performance scalability, energy efficiency, and system reliability, especially for data-intensive and large-model AI workloads. He has published around 100 papers in leading international conferences and journals, and has received multiple awards, including Best Paper Awards at the Great Lakes Symposium on VLSI (2021) and IEEE Transactions on Computers (2021), the CAS Outstanding Award for Technological Achievements Transformation (2019), and the Huawei Olympus Pioneering Award (2023).

Dr. Liu serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Emerging Topics in Computing, and as an \textbf{Area Chair for ICML 2027 and ICLR 2027}. He has served on the technical program committees of major design automation and FPGA conferences, including DAC, FPT, ATS, DFT, and DFTS, and as a reviewer for leading AI conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, and AAAI, as well as for IEEE journals such as TCAD, TVLSI, TPDS, TC, TECS, and TRETS. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and CCF, and a member of ACM.

\end{rSection}
